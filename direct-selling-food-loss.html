Direct Selling under Yield Uncertainty and Its Impact on Food Loss

Xin Geng and X. Wang

Submitted to Production and Operations Management.

Research Overview

This research studies direct selling in agricultural supply chains with uncertain production yields, focusing on how the availability and timing of a farmer's direct-selling channel affect production decisions and food loss in the farmer-retailer relationship.

Research topics: agricultural supply chains, direct selling, yield uncertainty, production decisions, food loss.
